Gamification System

Your resilience becomes your superpower. Earn XP, level up, and compete with the community.

XP & Level System

Earn XP for Every Action

Posting, commenting, liking, sharing, and even daily logins earn you XP. Every interaction counts toward your growth.

Level Up Through 100+ Tiers

Accumulate XP to level up and unlock new status levels, from Spark to Blaze and beyond. Watch your progress in real-time with celebratory level-up notifications.

Badges & Streaks

Daily Streaks

Every time you post, your daily streak grows. Maintain your streak to earn bonus XP and show your commitment to the community.

Achievement Badges

Unlock badges for milestones like your first post, first follower, 7-day streak, and more. Your badge collection is displayed on your profile as proof of your journey.

Weekly Leaderboards

Compete & Climb

The leaderboard resets every week, giving everyone a fresh chance to climb. See your weekly rank, compare your XP with others, and earn bragging rights as a top contributor.

Timed Challenges

Special Goals & Deadlines

Take on challenges with specific goals and deadlines. Join challenges to track your progress, earn bonus XP rewards, and unlock exclusive badges. Challenges range from posting streaks to community engagement goals.
