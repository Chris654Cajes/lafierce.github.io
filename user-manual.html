✍️ Creating Posts

Learn how to share your resilience story with the community.

1

Tap the Create Button

Look for the + button in the center of the bottom navigation bar.

2

Write Your Content

Share your story, experience, or words of encouragement. You can also add photos or images.

3

Add Tags

Add relevant tags to help others discover your post. Use keywords like #resilience, #growth, #motivation.

4

Post

Tap Post to share with the community. You'll earn XP for creating content!

Pro Tip

Be authentic! Your genuine story has the power to inspire others who may be going through similar challenges. Don't be afraid to be vulnerable.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about La-Fierce.

?

Is La-Fierce free?

Yes! La-Fierce is completely free to download and use. We believe everyone deserves access to a supportive community.

?

How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap the three dots on any post and select "Report". Our team reviews all reports promptly.

?

Can I change my username?

Currently, usernames cannot be changed after registration. Choose carefully when you sign up!

?

How does the XP system work?

XP is earned through positive engagement. Create posts, comment, like, share, and maintain streaks to level up!

?

Is my data safe?

We take privacy seriously. Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties without your consent.
