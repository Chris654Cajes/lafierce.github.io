The User Journey

A deep dive into the core experience.

01

Secure Entry

Start your journey with a clean, distraction-free welcome screen. Log in with your username and password, or sign up if you're new. We prioritize your privacy so you can focus on being authentic.

02

The Resilience Feed

Share your pivots and failures to inspire others. Like, comment, save, and share posts — and tap any avatar to visit that person's profile. Your bell icon shows real-time notification badges.

03

Deep Conversations

Tap into any post to read the full story and its comments. Every comment is filtered by our AI Sentiment Engine so only uplifting responses get through. Heart comments you connect with and send Cheers to the author.

04

Cheer for Each Other

The Men and Women of Inspiration section lets you send "Cheers" and faith-based support to those facing their own "Goliaths." Follow other users, send friend requests, and receive push notifications for every interaction.

05

AI-Powered Kindness

Our proprietary AI Sentiment Engine scans every comment in real-time to ensure your shares are met with support, not toxicity.

  • Allowed: Encouraging words like "Don't give up" or "You're amazing."
  • Blocked: Negative phrases or "hopeless" sentiments are automatically filtered.
06

Re-share & Amplify

Found a story that moved you? Re-share it to your own feed with your own caption. The original post is embedded so your followers get the full context — and the original author gets notified of every share.

07

Your Shares

The Shares tab keeps a dedicated log of every post you've amplified. Edit your caption anytime, like and comment on the embedded original, or remove a share — all from one focused view.

08

Manage Your Identity

Slide open the menu to access your Profile, edit your details, enter the Arena, manage Friend Requests, or switch between Light and Dark mode. Everything about your account is one tap away.

09

Direct Messaging

Message any La-Fierce member directly. Your inbox shows every active conversation with message previews and timestamps. Unread counts appear as a live badge on the tab — separate from your notification bell.

10

Real Conversations

Inside a conversation, messages flow naturally with clear sent/received bubbles. Start from a user's profile or the Messages tab — and keep the connection going beyond the public feed.

11

Search & Discovery

Find users, posts, and trending hashtags in real-time. Search results appear as you type with a 400ms debounce. Follow users directly from search results, or tap their avatar to visit their full profile.

12

Real-Time Notifications

Stay on top of every interaction — likes, shares, comments, friend requests, follows, and more. Unread notifications appear highlighted in red. Mark all as read in one tap and never miss a moment.

13

Badges & Streaks

Every time you post, your daily streak grows. Unlock badges for milestones like your first post, first follower, and more. Your streak and badge collection are displayed on your profile as living proof of your resilience.

14

Nominate a Success Story

Know someone fierce enough to be a La-Fierce Success Story? Search their username and nominate them with a reason. They receive an in-app notification and a push alert celebrating their recognition.

15

XP, Levels & Leaderboards

Every action earns XP — posting, commenting, liking, sharing, and daily logins. Accumulate XP to level up and climb the weekly leaderboard. Your rank and XP progress are tracked in real-time against the whole community.

16

Your Profile

Your profile displays your posts, follower count, and following count — all tappable to explore your network. Every story you've shared lives here as a permanent record of your journey.

17

Edit Your Identity

Update your profile picture, username, and gender easily. Security settings let you change your password directly from the same screen — keeping your account fully yours.

18

Dark Mode

Switch to Dark Mode for a fully immersive night-friendly experience. Every screen — from the feed to your profile — adapts to a sleek dark theme so you can stay fierce at any hour.

19

App Updates & Announcements

See what's new and what's coming next. The Updates screen keeps you informed with release notes, community announcements, and pending updates — so you're always in the loop as La-Fierce grows.
