The User Journey

A deep dive into the core experience.

01

Secure Entry

Start your journey with a clean, distraction-free welcome screen. Sign up with your username, email, and gender — or continue with Google. We prioritize your privacy so you can focus on being authentic.

02

The Resilience Feed

Available in both Light and Dark modes. Share your pivots and failures to inspire others. Like, comment, bookmark, react, and share posts — and tap any avatar to visit that person's profile.

03

Cheer for Each Other

The Men and Women of Inspiration section lets you send "Cheers" and faith-based support to those facing their own "Goliaths." Follow other users, send friend requests, and receive push notifications for every interaction.

04

Manage Your Identity

Update your profile picture, username, gender, and password easily. Your profile displays your Posts, Shares, follower count, and following count — all tappable to explore your network.

05

AI-Powered Kindness

Our proprietary AI Sentiment Engine scans every comment in real-time to ensure your shares are met with support, not toxicity.

  • Allowed: Encouraging words like "Don't give up" or "You're amazing."
  • Blocked: Negative phrases or "hopeless" sentiments are automatically filtered.
06

Direct Messaging

Message any La-Fierce member directly. Start a conversation from a user's profile or from the Messages tab. Unread message counts appear as a live badge on the tab — separate from your notification bell.

07

Search & Discovery

Find users, posts, and trending hashtags in real-time. Search results appear as you type with a 400ms debounce. Follow users directly from search results, or tap their avatar to visit their full profile.

08

Badges & Streaks

Every time you post, your daily streak grows. Unlock badges for milestones like your first post, first follower, and more. Your streak and badge collection are displayed on your profile as living proof of your resilience.

09

Nominate a Success Story

Know someone fierce enough to be a La-Fierce Success Story? Search their username and nominate them with a reason. They receive an in-app notification and a push alert celebrating their recognition.

10

XP & Level System

Every action you take earns XP — posting, commenting, liking, sharing, and even daily logins. Accumulate XP to level up and unlock new status levels, from Spark to Blaze and beyond. Watch your progress in real-time with level-up notifications that celebrate your growth.

11

Weekly Leaderboards

Compete with the community on a weekly basis. The leaderboard resets every week, giving everyone a fresh chance to climb. See your weekly rank, compare your XP with others, and earn bragging rights as a top contributor.

12

Timed Challenges

Take on special challenges with specific goals and deadlines. Join challenges to track your progress, earn bonus XP rewards, and unlock exclusive badges. Challenges range from posting streaks to community engagement goals.
