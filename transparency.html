Data Transparency

We only record what helps you grow. Nothing else.

Our Commitment

La-Fierce is built on the principle of Sovereign Resilience. Your data belongs to you. We do not scrape your contacts, we do not track your location outside the app, and we never access data from other applications on your device.

What We Record

  • Account Essentials: Username, Email, and Gender (to personalize your experience).
  • Social Interaction: Your posts, shares, comments, likes, reactions, bookmarks, follows, and friend connections within the community.
  • Direct Messages: Private conversations between users, stored securely and never read by our team.
  • Resilience Metrics: Your daily posting streak, earned badges, XP progress, level, and success nominations.
  • Notifications & Feedback: In-app notifications for your activity and any feedback or reports you voluntarily submit.
  • Gamification Data: XP ledger entries, leaderboard rankings, challenge progress, and level achievements to power your growth journey.
